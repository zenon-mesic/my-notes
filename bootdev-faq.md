### What is an Archmage Coin?

It's a [physical coin](https://x.com/wagslane/status/1778762286364024871?lang=en) that the boot.dev teams mails to anyone who has hit LV 100 on their platform.

## Discord

### How to ask a good question?

A few things to keep in mind:
- Do some basic research. Do not treat server members as Google or ChatGPT. Respect their time.
- Take some time to formulate your question properly. 0- to low-effort questions should expect to receive 0- to low-effort responses.
- Use [Discord Markdown](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/210298617-Markdown-Text-101-Chat-Formatting-Bold-Italic-Underline) to format your message.
- Make sure to use [code blocks](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/210298617-Markdown-Text-101-Chat-Formatting-Bold-Italic-Underline#h_01GY0DAKGXDEHE263BCAYEGFJA) when sharing code.

### What is the `/softban` command?

You can use the `/softban` command to flag a user who sent an inappropriate message. A notification will be sent to the group responsible for the Discord server and they will make a decision on how to address the reported user. Using `/softban` will not ban the user.

### How much XP is needed for each LV?

As of 9th of July, 2026, the formula is:

```
XP needed for next LV = Current LV * 80 + 320
```
Example:
If you are LV 19, the XP needed for LV 20 is `19 * 80 + 320 = 1840`.

### How do I get Karma points?

Boots (the official boot.dev Discord bot) will automatically give you:
- 5 karma points for every emoji reaction to your message
- 25 karma points when you are thanked in a message that directly responds to your message by using the "Reply" function
- 25 karma points when you thanked in a message in which you are tagged (with @ symbol)
- up to 200 karma points when a post in any of the forum channels, such as #help-lesson-python, is marked as solved. 200 karma points are automatically split among everyone who contributed based on the quantity of their contribution.

*Note*: Automated Karma rewarding has an internal cooldown.

### How do I get the Arcanum role?

Get:
- LV 100 (the title of Archmage)
- 16,386 karma points

### Are users with the Arcanum role boot.dev employees?

As a general rule no, but some team members do have the Arcanum role, among other roles.

### Why were magic links removed as a login method?

The boot.dev team explained it briefly in a [Discord message](https://discord.com/channels/551921866173054977/1485758807484272842/1485894681039605810).

### What is a Greater XP Potion and how do I get one?

A Greater XP Potion gives the same 25 % XP bonus as the regular potion, but lasts a full 72 hours. These can not be purchased, and are only dropped as a rare reward in chests.
